My Thoughts on Future Directions
Effectiveness: Develop system-level calibration and coordination methods that enhance the overall effectiveness of LLM-based simulations and optimization frameworks. This includes exploring feedback mechanisms that connect model behavior with organizational or operational objectives.

Efficiency: Design scalable and computationally efficient algorithms that maintain model performance while significantly reducing training and inference costs, especially in high-dimensional and multi-agent environments.

Robustness: Establish adaptive and resilient optimization mechanisms capable of handling uncertainty, distributional shifts, and noisy or incomplete data to ensure stable decision-making outcomes in dynamic systems.
